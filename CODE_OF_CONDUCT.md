# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about taste, not about the other person.
- Say what you mean, and say it about the work rather than about the person doing it.
- Accept that a maintainer may not merge your pull request, and that they may not explain why.
- Give credit when someone else's work or idea helped you.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or anywhere else the project
  is discussed.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour, including sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and in any space where someone is
taking part in the project.

## Reporting

If something happens that breaks these rules, report it to the maintainer at the address on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/ishaanrajiv), or use GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/ishaanrajiv/tern/security/advisories/new). Reports are
handled privately. You will not be asked to confront the other person, and you will not be asked to
relay details of the report to them.

The maintainer will acknowledge a report, decide what to do, and tell you what they decided where
they can. Retaliating against someone for reporting is itself a breach of this policy.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for enforcing these rules and may take any action they judge
appropriate, from a quiet word to removing a contribution or a participant. They will try to keep
it proportionate and to focus on repairing the harm rather than punishing.
